Verdict ❌ No SLA ✅ Our Choice ⚠️ Overkill ⚠️ Overkill
Why Standard C1 (4 GB · ~$50/month)? Our Redis usage is lightweight — job state per bid is ~1–2 KB, pub/sub for WebSocket notifications, and result cache for repeat page drawings. 4 GB is well above what we need. Standard gives us 2-node replication (HA) and a 99.9% SLA which is essential since Celery's chord() fan-in and real-time WebSocket push both depend on Redis being available.
⚠️ Basic is not an option for production. It has no replication and no SLA — if the single node goes down, Celery workers lose their result backend, fan-in breaks, and WebSocket push stops. Always use Standard or above in production.
Why NOT Premium? Clustering and persistence are not needed — Redis is our cache and pub/sub broker, not a primary data store. Data loss on restart is acceptable since Azure SQL is the source of truth. VNet isolation is already provided by the existing FieldJetX VNet.
Why NOT Enterprise? Redis modules (RediSearch, RedisJSON etc.) are not used in our stack. Extremely expensive for our scale.
