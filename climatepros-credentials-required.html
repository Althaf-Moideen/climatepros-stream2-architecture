Verdict ❌ Avoid ✅ Our Choice ⚠️ Overkill
Why Standard? It has every feature our architecture needs — dead-letter queue (failed jobs after 3 attempts), lock timeout (5 min), max delivery count (3), KEDA autoscaling, and async job dispatch. At 80–500 bids/month our total operations are well within the first 13 million free — so we pay just the $10/month base fee.
⚠️ Important: Upgrading between tiers (Basic → Standard → Premium) requires creating a new namespace and migrating — you cannot upgrade in place. Start with Standard from day one to avoid migration overhead later.
Why NOT Basic? No dead-letter queue — failed jobs are lost silently. No sessions or transactions. Not suitable for any production use.
Why NOT Premium? VNet is already handled by the existing FieldJetX VNet. Geo-DR not needed at our volume. 67× more expensive ($677 vs $10/month). Premium only becomes cost-competitive at 1.36 billion operations/month — we are nowhere near that.
